Reporting a problem

To help me fix the issue faster, please include:

  • The app name and app version
  • Your device model (for example, iPhone 15) and iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • What happened and the steps to reproduce it
  • Screenshots or a screen recording, if possible

ScannerApp - Scan & OCR

App Store · Privacy Policy

Where are my documents stored?

On your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, copies are also stored in your own iCloud Drive. I don't run any server that receives your documents, so I can't see or recover them. Turning on iCloud sync is the best way to keep a backup.

Does text recognition (OCR) need an internet connection?

No. Text recognition runs on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks.

How do I get Premium back on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy Premium, open ScannerApp, and use the Restore Purchases option.

SplitBill: Tip & Split Bill

App Store · Privacy Policy

Where are my bills and groups stored?

Only on your device. SplitBill has no account or sign-up, and your bills are never sent to a server.

Why aren't exchange rates updating?

Exchange rates need an internet connection. Check that you're online and that SplitBill is allowed to use mobile data (Settings → Mobile Service / Cellular). SplitBill Pro also lets you refresh rates manually.

How do I get SplitBill Pro back on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy SplitBill Pro, open SplitBill, and use the Restore Purchases option.

Purchases & refunds

All purchases are processed by Apple. I don't receive your payment details and can't issue refunds directly. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.
