Privacy Policy

SplitBill: Tip & Split Bill · Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version: SplitBill does not collect your data. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising. Your bills, groups, and history stay on your device. The app uses the internet only to download currency exchange rates.

This Privacy Policy explains how SplitBill: Tip & Split Bill (the "App"), developed by Heru Sulistya ("I", "me"), handles information.

1. Data stored on your device

The bills, items, tips, groups, history, and settings you enter are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to me or to any server I operate, and I cannot access them. Deleting the App removes this data, although it may still be included in device backups (such as iCloud Backup) that you control.

2. Currency exchange rates

To convert currencies, the App connects to a third-party exchange-rate service and downloads current rates. This request does not include your bills, amounts, or any personal information. As with any internet request, the service may see your device's IP address as part of the normal connection.

3. Sharing receipts

When you share a split as a receipt image, the App uses the standard iOS share sheet. The image goes only to the app or person you choose; I do not receive a copy.

4. In-app purchases

SplitBill Pro is purchased through Apple. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. I receive confirmation that a purchase was made, but never your payment or billing details.

5. Analytics and advertising

The App does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

6. Children

The App is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights

Because the App does not collect personal data, I do not hold any of your data to access, correct, or delete. You can delete all App data at any time by deleting the App. If you have questions about your rights under laws such as Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022) or the EU GDPR, please contact me.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how data is handled, this page will be updated with a new "Last updated" date before that version is released.
